## What data is processed

### Server logs and statistics

- **Data:** the full IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the browser and the page you came from.
- **Purpose:** to run and protect the site, and to count visits in aggregate statistics, made only from these logs.
- **Legal basis:** the legitimate interest in a secure site and in knowing how many visits it gets.
- **Recipients:** Infomaniak, which hosts the site, in Switzerland, a country covered by a European Commission adequacy decision.
- **Retention:** as long as Infomaniak keeps the logs, at least 7 days. No copies are made.

### Emails received

- **Data:** your email address, your name and what you write.
- **Purpose:** to reply to you.
- **Legal basis:** the legitimate interest in replying to whoever writes.
- **Recipients:** Google, which stores the mailbox (Google Workspace).
- **Retention:** if you do not become a client, 3 years after your last contact.

### Conversation bookings

- **Data:** first name, last name, email, phone, the answers to the form's questions and the guests' emails.
- **Purpose:** to book and prepare the Conversation.
- **Legal basis:** the pre-contractual steps you ask for by booking.
- **Recipients:** Calendly, which handles the booking, and Google (Calendar and Meet), in the United States, under the Data Privacy Framework.
- **Retention:** if you do not become a client, 3 years after your last contact.

### Clients

- **Data:** the contact details of the people Julien works with, the contracts and the invoices.
- **Purpose:** to perform the contract and invoice.
- **Legal basis:** the performance of the contract and the legal accounting obligations.
- **Recipients:** Google (Google Workspace) and, when the law requires it, the accountant and the tax authorities.
- **Retention:** for as long as the business relationship lasts, then the legal periods (invoices and accounting records: 10 years).

### Testimonial authors

- **Data published:** the text, the first name, the job title and the company.
- **Purpose:** to show what people who worked with Julien say.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, given in writing.
- **Recipients:** anyone who visits the site, which is public, and Infomaniak, which hosts it.
- **Retention:** until you ask for its removal, by email to {email}. The Testimonial leaves the site with the next Publishing.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its rectification, its erasure, the restriction of processing and portability. You can object at any time to the processing based on legitimate interest (the logs and the emails) and withdraw the consent given for a Testimonial. For any request, write to {email}.

## Complaints

If you think your data is not handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority ([cnil.fr/fr/plaintes](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes)), or with the authority of the country where you live.
