# Julien Laffont's landing page

Page that presents the services of Julien Laffont, a freelance DevOps consultant, and leads CTOs of growing SaaS companies to book a conversation with him.

Issues and PRs are written in Portuguese, so each term also gives its Portuguese name (_Portuguese_).

## People

**CTO**:
The page's target audience: the CTO of a growing SaaS who starts facing regressions, instability, or performance and scaling limits.
_Portuguese_: CTO
_Avoid_: client (only after hiring), prospect, lead

**Visitor**:
Anyone who opens the page, whether a CTO or not.
_Portuguese_: Visitante
_Avoid_: user

## Page goal

**Conversation**:
The first call between a CTO and Julien, booked in an external tool. It is the action the page aims to bring about.
_Portuguese_: Conversa
_Avoid_: meeting, demo, call

**Booking tool**:
The external service where the CTO books the conversation with Julien.
_Portuguese_: Ferramenta de marcação
_Avoid_: scheduling tool, calendar

**Booking page**:
The booking tool page where the CTO picks the time of the conversation. There is only one, in French, for the three languages.
_Portuguese_: Página de marcação
_Avoid_: event type, event, Calendly link

## Service

**Block**:
Each of the four parts of Julien's service, which the CTO can hire separately or together: CI pipeline and code audit, containerisation, scalable infrastructure, observability.
_Portuguese_: Bloco
_Avoid_: module, phase, step (the steps of the collaboration)

**Promise**:
The page's main sentence: the result Julien delivers to the CTO, and how fast.
_Portuguese_: Promessa
_Avoid_: slogan, tagline, short message

**Designation**:
The short sentence next to Julien's name that says what he does and for whom. There is one per language. It is not the Promise.
_Portuguese_: Designação
_Avoid_: title, subtitle, tagline, slogan

**Guarantee**:
Julien's commitment on the Availability of the infrastructure he delivers, with the conditions the client meets and what Julien does if it fails.
_Portuguese_: Garantia
_Avoid_: Garantie Sans Régression, Garantie Sans Bug

**Availability**:
The percentage of time the infrastructure delivered by Julien is working, measured month by month.
_Portuguese_: Disponibilidade
_Avoid_: SLA (the contract between the CTO's SaaS and its users), uptime

**Step**:
Each step of a collaboration with Julien, in this order: Conversation, Diagnosis, Intervention proposal, Implementation, Delivery and handover.
_Portuguese_: Etapa (Conversa, Diagnóstico, Proposta de intervenção, Implementação, Entrega e transferência)
_Avoid_: phase (the page phases), block

**Diagnosis**:
The Step in which Julien analyses the CTO's system, after the Conversation and before the Intervention proposal.
_Portuguese_: Diagnóstico
_Avoid_: audit (the code audit is part of the first Block)

**Intervention proposal**:
The Step in which Julien, after the Diagnosis, tells the CTO which Blocks he will do, in what timeframe and at what fixed price.
_Portuguese_: Proposta de intervenção
_Avoid_: quote, devis, commercial proposal

**Lower-impact hosting**:
A cloud or hosting provider that Julien can suggest to the CTO because it uses less energy or fewer resources than the others. No hosting has zero impact.
_Portuguese_: Alojamento com menor impacto ambiental
_Avoid_: green server, green hosting, responsible hosting

**Data sovereignty**:
A CTO's requirement that data stays in the European Union, with a European provider, or in a SecNumCloud-qualified service when the sector requires it. It goes beyond where the data is stored.
_Portuguese_: Soberania dos dados
_Avoid_: data in the EU (only says where the data is), sovereign cloud (the providers' marketing label)

**Testimonial**:
A quote from someone who worked with Julien, published with the author's written permission, with their first name, job title and company.
_Portuguese_: Testemunho
_Avoid_: rating, avis (the names on Malt), review, recommendation

## Page phases

**First review**:
The first complete version of the page, in Portuguese only, reviewed with Julien before the Commercial launch.
_Portuguese_: Primeira validação
_Avoid_: beta, MVP

**Commercial launch**:
The publication of the page at devops.julienlaffont.com in French, English and Portuguese.
_Portuguese_: Lançamento comercial
_Avoid_: go-live, launch (unqualified)

**Publishing**:
Sending the built site to the Infomaniak hosting, which puts it online at devops.julienlaffont.com. It happens after each merge into `main`.
_Portuguese_: Publicação
_Avoid_: deploy, upload, launch

## Languages

**Default language**:
The language a Visitor sees when opening devops.julienlaffont.com without choosing one: French.
_Portuguese_: Língua por defeito
_Avoid_: main language

**Reference language**:
The language in which the texts are written and approved with Julien before being translated: Portuguese. French and English are translations of it. Testimonials are the exception: they start from the author's text, and translations are marked as such.
_Portuguese_: Língua de referência
_Avoid_: original language

## Legal pages

**Legal information**:
The page that identifies Julien as a provider: name, status, registration, address and contact details.
_Portuguese_: Informação legal
_Avoid_: legal notice, mentions légales (only as the French title), terms and conditions

**Privacy policy**:
The page that explains which personal data of Visitors, CTOs and Testimonial authors is processed, by whom and what for.
_Portuguese_: Política de privacidade
_Avoid_: cookie policy, GDPR

## Visits and privacy

**Origin**:
Where the Visitor came to the page from: LinkedIn, Google, an email, direct access or a campaign.
_Portuguese_: Origem
_Avoid_: source, referrer, channel

**Cookie notice**:
The consent request shown to the Visitor before the page stores or reads information in their browser.
_Portuguese_: Aviso de cookies
_Avoid_: cookie banner, consent pop-up
